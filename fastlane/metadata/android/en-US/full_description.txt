TravelMate is a free, open-source multimodal public transit planner for Hyderabad, India — built by Swecha as a civic technology project.

Plan your daily commute across Hyderabad Metro, MMTS suburban rail, and TSRTC city buses — all in one app, with no internet required for routing.

KEY FEATURES

🚌 Multimodal Routing
Plan journeys combining Hyderabad Metro (Red, Blue, Green lines), MMTS suburban rail, 1,000+ TSRTC bus routes, and auto-rickshaw last-mile segments in a single trip.

🗺️ Live Map View
See your full route on an interactive map with live traffic, weather, and AQI (Air Quality Index) overlays.

🧭 Smart Commuter Strategies
Choose from six routing strategies — Fastest Arrival, Least Waiting, Metro Preferred, MMTS Preferred, Bus Preferred, and Local Commuter — each tailored for different travel needs.

🇮🇳 Telugu Language Support
Full app interface available in English and Telugu (తెలుగు). Over 180 station and stop names are translated.

📊 Mobility Analytics
View a city-wide GIS heatmap of Hyderabad transit coverage and stop density across all modes.

🚗 Community RidePool
Browse and offer shared rides with other commuters. Includes an integrated points and wallet system.

💬 Feedback
Submit feedback directly from the app to help improve Hyderabad's public transit experience.

🔒 Privacy First
All routing runs entirely on-device. No account, no login, no personal data collected or transmitted.

♿ Accessible
Screen reader support, ARIA landmarks, and keyboard navigation throughout.
