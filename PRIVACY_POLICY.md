# Privacy Policy — TravelMate

**Last updated:** June 2026
**Developer:** Swecha (CivicTech), Hyderabad, India

## What data we collect

TravelMate collects **no personal data**.

- No account or login required
- No name, email, or phone number collected
- No location data sent to any server
- No analytics or tracking SDKs included

## On-device storage only

Route history is saved **only on your device** using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Internet usage

TravelMate uses the internet only for:
- Loading map tiles (OpenStreetMap — no account required)
- Fetching live weather and AQI data from public APIs
- Geocoding search queries via Nominatim (no personal identifiers sent)

All transit routing (bus, metro, MMTS) runs **entirely on-device** using offline GTFS data.

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
